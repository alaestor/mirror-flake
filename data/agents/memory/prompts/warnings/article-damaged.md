A memory has missing, malformed, or mismatched managed files. Inspect
`memory status` and `memory path <id>`. Do not use the damaged article as evidence.
Restore it from Git, or move its directory outside the store's `articles/`
directory before recreating it. If metadata is intact, revision-checked
`memory remove` is also available, but permanently deletes supporting files.
Healthy articles remain available. Do not discard unrelated memories.
